Artsy returned to the same location as [last year’s offsite](https://photos.ashfurrow.com/artsy-offsite-2015), and I had a great time. I brought lots of film and made sure to have my camera with me everywhere, I got some great shots.

Orta and his T-Rex (used as a controller for his learn-to-program workshop). The dino has a coat made of aluminium foil because it turns out they wouldn’t conduct electricity without it.

![A black-and-white photo of a man wearing a Pikachu-themed snapback cap with ears and a grinning face, standing outdoors near trees.](07.jpg)

Lots of swimming, some zip lining, and just chilling in between workshops.

![Black-and-white photo of a water skier tumbling and splashing up spray on a lake, with a tow rope stretched behind and forested hills in the background.](09.jpg)
![Black-and-white photo of people relaxing on a wooden dock at a lake, with a large inflatable swan float and wooded hills in the sunlight behind.](10.jpg)

Even though we were supposed to unplug and have fun, you can see the finance team on the right wrapping up monthly expense reports. Now that’s dedication.

![Black-and-white photo of a young man in a t-shirt tossing a beanbag toward a cornhole board on grass, with trees and people behind him.](11.jpg)
![Black-and-white photo of two people working on laptops at a stone-pillared picnic shelter strung with lights, with others seated behind them.](12.jpg)

I know it’s cliched, but I’m so glad I got a chance to leave the city. I love New York, but I grew up in the country and a part of me will always be more comfortable there.

![Black-and-white photo of tall pine trunks casting long shadows across a grassy clearing, with a wooden bench in the distance.](13.jpg)
![Black-and-white photo of blooming black-eyed Susans with dark centers and drooping petals on thin stems.](14.jpg)
![Black-and-white photo of a tall slender tree on a grassy bank beside a still lake, with a wooded hillside behind it.](15.jpg#wide)

My favourite part of the offsite was getting to talk to people I normally wouldn’t work with in my day-to-day job. A lot of colleagues have told me the same thing.
