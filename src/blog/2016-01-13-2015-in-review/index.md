Our year began with an overnight ferry ride to Newcastle, in the UK. We had a marvellous time, but I think the images I like best were from the ferry ride itself.

![A lit lighthouse at the end of a stone breakwater stretches into dark choppy sea at dusk, with a town's lights along the distant shore.](01.jpg#wide)

The small, historic village of [Zaanse Schans](/blog/zaanse-schans/) sits just outside of Amsterdam. I used my new (to me) TLR and shot my first roll of Velvia. I’m glad I got to before Fuji stops making it for good.

![A wooden windmill with dark sails stands on the shore of calm blue water, its reflection visible below, with a second windmill in the distance.](02.jpg#wide)

[Paris](/blog/paris-in-winter/) was beautiful. After giving a well-received talk, we took an extra day to explore the city.

![A sepia photo of a stone arch bridge over a river, with birds flying above and a cobblestone quay in the foreground.](03.jpg#bleed)

We had a few weeks left in Amsterdam, and I made good use of it, experimenting primarily with [medium format](/blog/medium-format/).

![A black-and-white photo of a bare tree standing alone in a misty, open field, with more leafless trees fading into the fog behind it.](04.jpg)
![Black-and-white portrait of a smiling woman with glasses, wearing a chunky knit scarf and tweed coat outdoors among bare trees.](05.jpg)
![Black-and-white photo of low, frost-tipped ferns in the foreground, with a bare tree trunk and the sun glowing through mist behind them.](06.jpg#wide)

This photo really captures everything I love about the film aesthetic. I shot it on a shitty point-and-shoot and the fog just utterly confused the metre, underexposing it. But that just means that there’s grain, and you can still clearly see the trees and the figures of the couple and their dog.

![Two small figures walk across a dark, misty field toward a line of bare trees in black and white.](07.jpg#wide)

Our move to New York was… bumpy. But we survived, and settled into a cozy apartment. At first, I felt self-conscious, always carrying a camera. I looked like a tourist, but it doesn’t bother me so much now.

![Aerial view of a dense city of low brick buildings under heavy grey fog, with a green glass office tower in the centre.](08.jpg#wide)
![Black-and-white photo of a blurred subway train passing a platform, with dark steel support columns in the foreground.](09.jpg)
![A black-and-white street photo of a person in a puffer jacket holding an umbrella on a wet sidewalk, with steam rising beside a building entrance.](10.jpg)

We didn’t have a lot of time in [Istanbul](/blog/istanbul/), but our hosts graciously showed us around the city. We ended the day with a dinner on the water.

![Black-and-white view down a row of stone columns lit from below, reflected in still water on the floor of an underground cistern.](11.jpg)
![Black-and-white view from water up at a long suspension bridge's deck and tower, with a city skyline of high-rises across the strait.](12.jpg)
![Frayed mooring ropes tied to a ferry's hull, with a hazy city skyline across the water.](13.jpg#wide)

After Turkey, we stayed in [Berlin](/blog/spring-in-berlin/) with a few different friends. After seeing the cherry blossoms bloom in New York before leaving, we had the distinct pleasure of seeing them again in Berlin.

![Black-and-white view of a city skyline behind dark trees, with a tall slender TV tower under big cumulus clouds.](14.jpg)
![Cobblestone street lined with pink cherry trees in bloom and parked cars, including a red van on the right.](15.jpg#wide)

After Berlin, we visited [Hamburg](/blog/hamburg/) to stay with our friends there for a week. I spoke at a small developer meetup, and that weekend we rented a car to visit the sea.

![Black-and-white photo of four sailboats with numbered sails (3, 7, 5, 4) moored at the left, with more sailboats on a wide lake.](16.jpg#wide)

![A red-and-white commuter train curves along several parallel rail tracks, with green trees and a tall tower in the distance.](17.jpg#wide)

![Illuminated lakeside buildings and church spires reflected in still water at night.](18.jpg#wide)

The seemingly unending beaches of Sankt Peter-Ording were amazing; with nothing around, you could close your eyes and walk. But you wouldn’t get far until your brain jolted you awake, not used to this freedom.

And then a small sandstorm hit. I didn’t like exposing my camera to this abuse, but I think it was worth it.

![Two dark silhouettes walk side by side across a wide, pale sandy beach toward a distant horizon, leaving footprints behind them.](19.jpg#bleed)

[Murnau](/blog/murnau/) is gorgeous. After a two-day train ride (stopping in Frankfurt overnight), we stayed with our friend and his family. I have never regretted living in a city more than I did when I saw the Bavarian countryside.

![Calm turquoise lake lined by green forested hills, with pale mountain peaks in the distance under a blue sky.](20.jpg#wide)
![Green forested mountainside sloping down to a river, with snow-capped peaks rising under a blue sky.](21.jpg)
![A small weathered wooden hut with a rust-colored roof stands in a green meadow dotted with yellow wildflowers, with forested hills behind it.](22.jpg)

[New York](/blog/summer-in-new-york/) in the summer is amazing, just brimming with life. We walk through Central Park, around our neighbourhood, and I always made sure to have a camera with me.

![Black-and-white photo of a metal fire escape ladder on a brick wall, with striped sunlight and shadow falling across it.](23.jpg)
![Black-and-white view of a still pond ringed by dense trees, with tall city buildings rising in the misty background.](24.jpg)
![Black-and-white street scene of a fruit stand with pears and apples in crates, with shoppers and a van behind it on a busy city street.](25.jpg)
![Black-and-white aerial view of a dense city skyline of high-rise towers and low buildings, with a river and a hazy sun in the distance.](26.jpg)

![A white sailboat with its sails up on choppy water, with a city skyline of tall buildings along the distant shore under a soft pink and blue sky.](27.jpg#wide)

![A sunlit cluster of tall skyscrapers in a city, with a green-topped tower at right and older stone buildings in front.](28.jpg#wide)

One of these was taken in a botanical garden, and another on my way to work. This city has beauty all around it, but sometimes you have to look.

![Orange daylily with yellow center open on a thin stem beside an unopened bud, against a blurred green garden background.](29.jpg)
![A blue ceramic planter holding red geraniums and green foliage, set in a glasshouse full of palms and ferns.](30.jpg#wide)

About the point in the summer that New York started to get on my nerves, I participated in [Artsy’s offsite retreat](/blog/artsy-offsite-2015/). It was amazing to get away from everything, spend time with my colleagues, and relax near nature.

![Black-and-white photo of a woman seated on a chair on a grassy bank by a misty lake, with two more people near a small dock.](31.jpg)
![Black-and-white photo of tall conifer trunks rising from a forest floor with exposed roots, sunlight filtering through the trees behind.](32.jpg#wide)

Of course, at the end of the day, sometimes I’m not inspired to go outside and explore New York. Sometimes, [I struggle](https://ashfurrow.com/blog/depression-graphed/) to get the energy to do it. But in those times, I always have my cats.

![A black-and-white photo of a tabby cat with a white muzzle lying with its head resting on the edge of a sofa, eyes half closed.](33.jpg#wide)
