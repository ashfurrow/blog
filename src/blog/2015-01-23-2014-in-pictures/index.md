## "Oh my god, amsterdam"

Amsterdam is, as far as I can tell, the most beautiful city in the world. [Arriving in mid-February](/blog/moving-to-amsterdam-photos/), the city was already alive and waiting to be shot. I tried to avoid the obvious tourist shots to give mine something meaningful, but in a few instances, I [couldn’t help myself](/blog/amsterdam/).

## "OMG film"

So at this point, I was shooting about half-and-half digital and film. I had a Canon 5D MkIII, an _awesome camera_, but I didn’t take it out for over a month after moving to Amsterdam. Instead, I was using a small Fuji X100S – a “worse” camera, but one that was getting me better pictures. I sold the 5D and continued to shoot more and more on film. Below is one of the last digital photos I took in the first half of the year.

## "it's still February?"

So Spring happens in the Netherlands _really_ early. Like, it’s already happening _now_. Coming from the frigid wasteland of Toronto, this was unexpected.

## "This baguette is amazing."

Shortly after moving to Amsterdam, I returned to North America to teach a course at [Treehouse](http://teamtreehouse.com) in [Orlando](/blog/analogue-orlando/). My host, Amit, showed me around to some awesome food, including the best baguette sandwich I’ve had to date.

## "What do you mean there's no water?"

Late March, we left for a few days to Maastricht. Bathroom renovations were to be completed while we were away. Upon returning, we had no toilet, no bathroom, and no running water at all. We wrote an angry email to our landlord, then booked a pair of train tickets to Paris for the next morning.

![Black-and-white photo looking up at the iron lattice structure and arches of the Eiffel Tower against a bright sky.](11.jpg#bleed)

## "On a une réservation." "Under which name?"

Booking a trip to Paris was kind of a gamble, since I was unemployed. I had been interviewing with [Artsy](http://www.artsy.net) and, while in Paris, received an official job offer. We celebrated that evening. I’d start in four days.

(It would be more than a month until we had a functioning shower, and still another week before we would even have water.)

Some friends were gathering in [Innsbruck, Austria](/blog/innsbruck-austria/) for a weekend of gezelligheid. We enjoyed amazing vistas, jazz brunch, and Mario Kart-ing down the face of a mountain.

## "... Shit."

I visited Art Basel, the biggest, most-importentest art fair in the world. It was my first art fair. So I mostly listened. And watched. And admired. So many amazing things to see and feel. It was a [deeply profound](http://ashfurrow.com/blog/reflections-on-art-basel-2014/) experience.

It was an unforgettable experience, which is fortunate since I shot the entire experience on film which I almost entirely lost to a bottle of expired film developer.

![Red sculpture of a zigzag line rising into a large upward-pointing arrow against a pale wall.](17.jpg#bleed)

## Things Happened

The Summer found me slipping into depression again. This time, it would get [worse](http://ashfurrow.com/blog/depression-sucks/) than it had ever been. I would not leave the apartment for days at a time. Trips to Copenhagen, Warsaw, Kraków all passed without a meaningful photograph made. It wouldn’t be until August that I’d feel excited about photography, and until October for me to begin to feel like myself again.

Through it all, I counted on the unwavering support of my wife Ashley and our two cats. They were with me when I was most alone.

![A black-and-white photo of a tabby cat lying on a paisley-patterned blanket, looking toward the camera.](18.jpg#bleed)

## "I can't believe this city gets more beautiful."

Returning to Amsterdam in the beginning of Autumn meant beautiful, beautiful colours shining on the unspeakably gorgeous backdrop of four century-old canals.

![Moored houseboats and a barge line a canal in Amsterdam, with green trees and brick townhouses along the bank.](19.jpg#bleed)

We discovered Amstelpark late in the Summer and it was gorgeous. A park so large it has a monorail to transport patrons from the petting zoo to the miniature hedge maze.

## "I don't even."

We lived in Transvaalbuurt for months before we noticed this peculiar anachronism tucked away in a corner of the neighbourhood. I don’t know how long it’s been there, but I hazard to guess a “really long time.” Moss practically oozes from the trunk and the windows are opaque except for the one spot curious passersby have rubbed to peek inside.

![An old, faded grey vintage sedan with round headlamps and a chrome grille, parked on a brick street lined with autumn leaves.](24.jpg#bleed)

## "French Fry Pizza?"

Our visit to Milan was all too short, but things were starting to get very busy at work. We got to spend a day walking around the beautiful, historic architecture.

![Black-and-white view up through a glass-and-iron vaulted roof over an ornate arcade with decorated balconies and painted panels.](29.jpg#bleed)

## "Is moscow traffic. What can do?"

We took a cab from the airport to the hotel and my goodness I have never seen traffic that bad. Our cab driver explained in broken English that it does, in fact, get much worse. Nevertheless, we had an [amazing time in this city](/blog/moscow/). And we took the subway back to the airport.

## "Happy revolution day! right?"

I was fortunate enough to be in [Minsk in November](/blog/minsk/), on the one hundredth anniversary of the October Revolution of 1914. I was at a conference where everyone spoke Russian, so I spent most of my day reading Wikipedia articles on the rich and complex history of Belarus, Russia, and the Soviet Union.

## "Pass me the film camera. No, the other film camera."

Arriving back in late [Autumn](/blog/autumn-in-amsterdam/), I was shooting mostly film. I didn’t know it, but I would continue to use more and more black and white than colour film. I was mainly shooting on a Leica M3 with a 50mm Summicron.

![Black-and-white street scene of two people walking along a tree-lined brick path past a parked cargo bicycle and rows of townhouses.](35.jpg#bleed)

Winter found us taking frequent walks, and I made sure always to have a camera (and, usually, a tripod). We enjoyed scenes all around this beautiful city, met other photographers, and had a great time.

## "Boring route or interesting route?" "This is a 4x4." "Let's go."

We took a drive a few kilometres through dunes of volcanic sand to reach this wreckage of a 1973 plane crash. The US Navy crew survived, but the fuselage has endured decades of wear from the wind and the graffiti.

![Black-and-white photo of a crashed propeller airplane fuselage lying on dark sand, its nose section tilted and windows broken out.](53.jpg#bleed)

## "Let's do this again."

2014 was an awesome year for us. We’re already excited about 2015, which starts with another transatlantic move to New York.
