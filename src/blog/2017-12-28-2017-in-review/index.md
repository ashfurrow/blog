This photo was actually an accident! I was resting my camera on a balcony railing (I didn't bring a tripod, oops) and I was doing the long exposure using the shutter button (I didn't bring a shutter release cable, oops). And as I lifted my finger from the camera, I nudged everything slightly. Since the railing wasn't flat, the camera only changed vertical pitch, and it made this awesome photo! I cursed myself at the time but once I got the film developed, I really like the results.

## Back in New YOrk, Baby!

So I'd taken colour film to Australia, and then Switzerland and Hungary, which is three trips in a row. That's unusual for me – I've shot mostly on B&W for three years now. I noticed how I'd be drawn to the colour aspects of photography, so when I got back to New York I kind of just embraced it. The rest of the year is entirely in colour.

Starting with this photo which, again, was an accident that turned out really cool. It's actually not a double-exposure, but the frame next to it on the film had been accidentally exposed directly to light for hours. This extreme exposure bled over into the next frame, which I later used to make this photo.
