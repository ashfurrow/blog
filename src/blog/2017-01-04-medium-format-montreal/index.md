These were all taken on my TLR using TMax 400 film. I wanted to mix things up a bit, so I brought an old camera I don't use often and only black and white film. I loved the city, my time there, and the photos I got to take. There are a bunch that didn't make the cut, and even if I'm not proud of them artistically, I still really enjoyed taking them.
