My 2016 photography started off a bit ominously: a [blizzard](/blog/snowed-in/) hit NYC so my wife and I headed out into the streets. I had a blast, met some other photographers, and got the new year off to a great start despite the weather.

Next up was my around-the-world trip from NYC to Lisbon ([colour](/blog/lisbon-in-springtime/), [black&white](/blog/lisbon-in-monochrome/)) to Tokyo, and back to NYC. I spoke at conferences in Lisbon and Tokyo on iOS development, and made sure to make time for photography. The trip was two weeks long, so I made sure to bring an adequate and varied supply of film.

Back in NYC, I started focusing on some different themes: looking for striking shadows, strong lines, and umbrellas. I dunno what it is about umbrellas, but they just look so cool! Look forward to an umbrella photo post before too long.

It was about this point that I bought a [large format camera](https://ashfurrow.com/blog/large-format-photography/), a beast of a camera that shoots 4x5 inch negatives. It's not the easiest to shoot with, but it's oddly satisfying. Looking forward to using it more once the evening sun comes back.

Finally, back in NYC I made sure to grab some photos of the foliage before winter set in. Central Park was a great place for photos and people-watching, I definitely need to visit it more often.

2016 has been for me, like more years, a mixed bag. Lots of good things, lots of bad things. I'm starting to accept that things will never be perfect and to make the best of sub-ideal situations. Expressing myself through photography definitely helps.
