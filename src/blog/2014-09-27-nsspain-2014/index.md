## Getting to LogroñO

This is the story of our trip to Logroño for [NSSpain](http://nsspain.com/2014/). Getting there was awesome. We flew to Bilbao then took a bus to Logroño and got to see the Spanish countryside and mountains.

We packed lots of cameras and lots of film, including a Polaroid with film from the [Impossible Project](https://www.the-impossible-project.com). Also brought a tripod that I never got around to actually using. After arriving, we were joined by lots of friends and colleagues and the next morning we headed to the hackathon.
